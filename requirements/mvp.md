Required first four; remaining headers required but values may be blank with explicit unchecked warnings. ISO8601 date-times with seconds and Z or ±HH:MM; no timezone guessing. Half-open booked intervals; buffer belongs after each job. Capacity setting is crew-hours per UTC day, explicit assumption, not person-hours. Maximum 2MiB/2000 jobs.
